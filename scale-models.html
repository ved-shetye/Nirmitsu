Engineering Excellence

Our Scale Model Fabrication Process

We combine digital precision 3D fabrication with traditional model-making craftsmanship to ensure structural accuracy, crisp micro-details, and seamless aesthetic appeal.

1. 3D Data & CAD Extraction

We process BIM, Revit, AutoCAD, and Rhino files, converting complex architectural digital data into 3D-printable physical components.

2. Hybrid 3D Printing & CNC

Utilizing SLA resin, SLS nylon 3D printing, and CNC routing for sub-millimeter accuracy on intricate structural facades.

3. Artisan Detailing & LEDs

Airbrush finishing, realistic foliage landscaping, and micro-wired smart LED circuits for realistic ambient lighting.

4. Acrylic Display & Shipping

Custom UV-blocking acrylic dust covers, integrated pedestals, and shock-resistant flight crates for safe global transit.
